### 9.2 Effects research (literature)

**TIM4 blockade / KD — antitumor axis (often beneficial in cavity/TAM contexts)**
- Blockade of TIM-4–AMPKα1–autophagy improves antigen presentation and augments chemo-triggered CTL (Baghdadi *Immunity* 2013); siRNA to TIM-4 used experimentally on macrophages
- Tim-4 blockade enhances anti-PD-1 and adoptive T cell therapy in peritoneal models by stopping Tim-4+ macrophages from sequestering PS+ CD8 T cells (Chow et al.)
- Context-dependent: early TIM4 on cDC1 can *support* cross-presentation; advanced TAM TIM4 often tolerogenic — **site matters**

**Tumor-cell TIM4**
- On glioma/LN-18 and other tumors, TIM4 can be pro-growth; knockdown/overexpression shifts apoptosis and clonogenicity (Li 2016)
- Aligns with breaking TIM4↔ANXA2→PI3K/AKT→L-OPA1 OXPHOS path in our model

**CSF / pineal siRNA delivery (feasibility)**
- Lipid–siRNA conjugates in CSF achieve durable multi-region knockdown (months) via perivascular transport
- AAV and CSF-route vectors accumulate in **pineal gland / pinealocytes** after brain, spinal, or ICV injection — pineal is a real sink for CSF-delivered nucleic acid vectors
- Primary culture protocols exist for siRNA knockdown in rat pineal cells (melatonin-pathway studies)

**Implication for C-v2:** Recess-restricted siTIM4 is mechanistically coherent: reduces local docking + may blunt ectopic TIM4 growth/OXPHOS signaling; CSF path can reach pinealocytes; must limit off-target KD in other CNS APCs that need TIM4 for surveillance.
